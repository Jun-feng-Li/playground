## S3: square denominators

Source: A. Dujella, M. Kazalicki, V. Petricevic, Rational Diophantine sextuples with square denominators, J. Number Theory 205 (2019), 340-346; DOI 10.1016/j.jnt.2019.06.006; arXiv:1903.02805.
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.02805
Actual host reading in this round: abstract and bibliographic information.
Abstract-level result: infinitely many sextuples have reduced denominators that are integer squares. Square denominators do not imply that any denominator equals 1, so this result alone does not give two integer entries.
